<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-content">
    <p>As part of our commitment to helping veterans and first responders rebuild from the ground up, we’ve partnered with Modern Athlete Strength Systems to offer access to <strong>Warfighter: Mobility &amp; Stability</strong>, a 12-week program built to restore proper movement, joint health, and functional strength. This is intended to be performed as a bolt-on to your current training program and can be performed at the beginning or end of your training session, or as a separate session.</p>
    <h3>What’s Included</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>12 weeks of expertly programmed mobility, stability, and foundational strength work</li>
      <li>Lifetime access to the program via the TrainHeroic app</li>
      <li>Daily sessions that can be completed in 10–15 minutes</li>
      <li>Instructional videos for every movement</li>
      <li>Designed by a Doctor of Physical Therapy and board-certified orthopedic specialist</li>
    </ul>
    <h3>Special Offer for Our Supporters</h3>
    <p>This program is available to anyone, whether you’re part of our nonprofit or not. A portion of each purchase directly supports Operation Antifragile and helps fund the free coaching, training, and support for those in our program.</p>
    <p>This program provides five sessions per week, each lasting approximately 10–15 minutes, focused on developing joint mobility and stability. Specifically designed to address the demands of Warfighters and first responders, it focuses on hip rotation and groin mobility and strength, spinal flexion, extension, and rotation, trunk and hip stability, plus foot and ankle mobility and strength.</p>
    <p>Our forum function is available on this program so you can communicate directly with Dr. Mraz along with other athletes on the program as you head toward better joint mobility and overall stability.</p>
