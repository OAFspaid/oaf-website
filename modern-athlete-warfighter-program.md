    <p>At Operation Antifragile, we believe physical training is a foundational pillar of rebuilding a strong, resilient life. That’s why we’ve partnered with Modern Athlete Strength Systems to provide our members with access to Warfighter, a premier strength and conditioning program designed by Division 1 strength and conditioning coaches and Special Operations veterans.</p>
    <h3>For OAF Members</h3>
    <p>If you’re accepted into the Operation Antifragile program, you’ll receive full, free access to Warfighter. This program was built specifically for those committed to becoming stronger, both physically and mentally. Training is delivered daily and focuses on functional strength, conditioning, and real-world readiness.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>Daily strength and conditioning workouts</li>
      <li>Expert coaching from top-tier performance staff</li>
      <li>Scalable programming for all ability levels</li>
      <li>Instructional videos and technique support</li>
      <li>A community of hundreds of athletes committed to the same mission: getting better</li>
    </ul>
    <h3>Want to Support the Cause and Get Stronger?</h3>
    <p>For those who aren’t enrolled in Operation Antifragile but still want to support our mission, you can join the Warfighter team through our unique OAF link and use promo code <strong>ANTIFRAGILE</strong> for 15% off your monthly membership, for life.</p>
    <p>A significant portion of every subscription through this partnership is donated directly to Operation Antifragile, helping us continue to provide coaching, resources, and training to veterans, first responders, and those rebuilding from trauma.</p>
