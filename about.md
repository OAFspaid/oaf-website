    <h2>Our Mission: Building Strength Through Adversity</h2>
    <p>At Operation Antifragile, we are committed to empowering veterans and first responders to embrace challenges, gain strength, and thrive in the face of adversity. Founded by veterans and first responders, our organization is designed to help those who serve or have served transition into civilian life or better balance their demanding careers with their personal well-being. We believe that true strength doesn’t just withstand stress—it grows stronger because of it.</p>
