  <div class="section-content">
    <h2>Our Mission: Building Strength Through Adversity</h2>
    <p>At Operation Antifragile and The Antifragile Foundation, we are committed to empowering veterans and first responders to embrace challenges, gain strength, and thrive in the face of adversity. Founded by veterans and first responders, our organization is designed to help those who serve or have served transition into civilian life or better balance their demanding careers with their personal well-being. We believe that true strength doesn’t just withstand stress—it grows stronger because of it.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-content">
    <h2>What Does It Mean to Be Antifragile?</h2>
    <p>The concept of being “antifragile” goes beyond resilience. It’s about thriving under pressure and using stress as a catalyst for growth and improvement. At the Antifragile Foundation, we teach our members how to cultivate strength—both mental and physical—in every aspect of their lives. Through our comprehensive programs, we provide the tools to help you not just recover but excel in the face of life’s challenges.</p>
  </div>
</section>

    <h2>Our Story</h2>
    <p>Operation Antifragile was born out of a deep understanding of the challenges faced by veterans and first responders. Our founders, who have personally experienced the difficulties of military transitions and the demanding nature of first responder careers, wanted to create a space where others could find the strength to thrive. By combining tactical expertise with health and wellness training, we’ve created a program that empowers individuals to build strength—inside and out.</p>
