    <h2>Why Nutrition Matters</h2>
    <p>For veterans transitioning out of the military and first responders navigating the high demands of their careers, maintaining good health can often take a backseat. However, proper nutrition is essential to building strength, recovering from stress or injury, and promoting long-term well-being. What you put into your body impacts every aspect of your performance, from energy levels to mental clarity.</p>
    <p>At Operation Antifragile, we focus on teaching you how to make the best choices for your unique needs. Our approach isn’t about restrictive diets or quick fixes; it’s about finding a sustainable, balanced way to fuel your body that promotes overall wellness.</p>
